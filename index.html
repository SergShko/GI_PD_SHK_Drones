How this was collected

Scope: drones used by criminal actors in a European country, 2021-01-01 to 2026-10-29, one row per event. State or hybrid incidents (airports, military sites), police use of drones and extremist plots are excluded and listed below.

Discovery: Google News RSS queries in 24 languages across 29 country editions, half-year windows; national court databases; parliamentary written-question APIs; official press rooms; agent-run searches per country. Every query is logged.

Each incident, statistic and claim carries a verbatim quote. A script re-fetches every source and checks that the quote is present; rows whose quote could not be re-found are flagged, not removed.

Evidence tier is descriptive: A court record; B official statement or primary video; C media citing an official source; D media without official source; E secondary only.
